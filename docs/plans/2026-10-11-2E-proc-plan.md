**Goal:** Lower every `$proc` node to ordinary cells exactly as Yosys 0.55 `proc -norom -noopt` does on the same RTLIL:
- decision trees become `$mux`/`$pmux` trees with `$eq`/`$reduce_or` selects;
- edge syncs become `$dff`/`$adff`/`$aldff`/`$dffsr`;
- combinational syncs become connections or `$dlatch`.

Each module is lowered completely or not at all, and `proc` ends with 2F's `opt_expr -keepdc` unless `--noopt`. With it land:
- IR change D: the eleven word flip-flop and latch cells, which 2F also needs;
- the shared `src/passes/ffinit.[ch]` and `src/passes/budget.[ch]`;
- the tools `proc-diff`, `yosys-phase`, `netlist-compare --implicit-models`, `elab-equiv --pass proc` and `heavy-run`;
- 48 construct rows in both latch modes;
- micro, VTR and Koios sweeps, a generator profile and `bench_proc`.

Not here: `opt` (2F), `proc_rom` (Parmys runs `-norom`), and the exit splice (2F's S-P).

**Architecture:** `src/passes/proc/` is one pass whose internal phases run in Yosys's order.
- Each `$proc` body is decoded once (`pw.c`) into a mutable working form that mirrors `RTLIL::Process`. Signals are runs of `odin3_pw_bit` in one `bits` vector; cases, switches and syncs are index forests.
- Step A (clean, rmdead, prune, arst, the clock pre-check) edits only that form, so a module with any error is left untouched.
- Steps B–D lower each process: mux trees, the module-wide latch step, flip-flops. They build cells through one helper and record connections in a union-find net map; the connections become IR merges at two points only (M1, M2).
- Step E deletes the nets the deleted nodes strand. Step F calls 2F's `odin3_opt_expr_run` with `keepdc`.
- Every walk uses an explicit stack. `--stop-after` re-encodes the working form (`pw_encode.c`) for bisecting against Yosys.

## Global Constraints

- **Code standard.**
  - C17 with the `-Werror` flag set.
  - Functions ≤ 60 lines, CCN ≤ 15, ≤ 5 parameters; request structs beyond that.
  - **No recursion in `src/`**: every phase walks with an explicit stack of `(case or switch index, position)` frames, and `check_signal`, quickcheck, `find_mux_feedback` and the fix-up use worklists. Test helpers (`proc_build`) may recurse.
