## Deviations

5.4a (implementing lane):
- `odin3_hdl_loc_parse(text, out, cap)` returns the count (all or nothing) instead of taking an `n`
  out parameter, so a count over the cap reports what was dropped (D-7).
- `odin3_hdl_locs` takes a context (`odin3_hdl_ctx`: first-leaf cache and the once-per-file warnings)
  and returns a struct; `odin3_prov_leaf` and `odin3_prov_location` of `backends/prov_name.h` are
  gone (their two users call `odin3_hdl_first_record`/`odin3_hdl_first`); `odin3_prov_name` takes the context.
- `odin3_hdl_restore` takes a request struct (the 5-parameter limit) and returns `{prov, capped}`.
- `odin3_hdl_name_vtr_decode` undoes VB-10 one step; `netlist_compare.py` repeats it to a fixed point.
- A record with a hierarchy but no location no longer gives `hier/name` in provenance names (W-2:
  only HDL locations name an object); `test_prov_names_hier_only` changed.
- `odin3_blif_write_opts` gained `src_attr` (positional initialisers in `test_blif_vtr.c`,
  `builtin.c`, `writer.c` got `, false`).
- `dump_locs` registered last in the built-in table, so the registry-order test is unchanged.
- The IR-13 sentence and the "Names in output" HDL-only sentence of `docs/IR.md` are in 5.4a (Task 3).
- The HDL walk is memoised per provenance record (first 8 distinct locations, composed over
  parents), `odin3_hdl_restore` always makes a DERIVED record (a single parent when the reader has
  no record of its own), a location with line 0 is never printed, and `dump_locs` escapes `\\`, `|`
  and newline in its first three fields (review of 664708a9).
- `/run-micro` found no micro goldens at its path (`golden/<arch>/micro/...`): nothing compared.
