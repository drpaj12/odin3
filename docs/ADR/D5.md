# D5: Hard-block inference by subgraph isomorphism

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-08

## Context
Inference must scale to DSP cascades, MACs, and matmul tiles, and the same engine must serve RE
(spec §2 rationale, §1 thread 1 and 2).

## Decision
Hard-block inference is subgraph-isomorphism based over IR fragments, with a cost model.
Patterns are IR, not text macros.

## Consequences
Positive:
- One matcher, two directions: forward (patterns are target hard blocks, seeded at anchor cells
  such as `$mul`) and backward `raise` (generic word-level structures) (spec §8).
Negative:
- Needs an algorithm and a pattern format of real complexity; spec §8 names VF2-style matching
  with anchor seeding, width-agnostic matching, and local-equivalence verification (ABC on the
  cone) before commit.
- Spec §12: the human reviews all mapping algorithms.
Forces on later phases:
- Phase 4: matcher v1, partial mapping (spec §7, §12).
- Phase 6: `raise` reuses the matcher.
- Spec §7: patterns are an IR fragment in a small textual DSL with wildcard widths, optional
  ports, and a cost; overlap resolution is maximum-cover with cost tie-break. The DSL is a
  serialization of IR fragments (interpretation - confirm).
- Plugins may register matcher patterns through the C ABI (spec §15.3).
