# D2: Owned Verilog-2005 grammar; SystemVerilog and VHDL via adapters

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-08

## Context
Odin II has a Bison/Flex Verilog grammar that Odin III evolves. slang (SystemVerilog) and GHDL
(VHDL) are not Bison/Flex and "would take years to re-create" (spec §2). A common AST is the
contract between front ends and the IR (spec §4.5).

## Decision
- Odin III owns a Bison/Flex Verilog-2005 grammar, evolved from Odin II's.
- SystemVerilog arrives through an adapter: slang to Odin III AST.
- VHDL arrives through a GHDL subprocess (`ghdl --synth`) producing Verilog, which then goes
  through the Odin III Verilog front end.
- Adapters preserve the common-AST contract.

## Consequences
Positive:
- No multi-year parser re-creation for SV and VHDL.
- VQM needs no new parser: the Verilog grammar plus the Altera primitive library (spec §4.4).
Negative:
- Dependence on slang's and GHDL's coverage and name stability (VHDL provenance relies on a
  GHDL-emitted name map, spec §4.3).
- The slang adapter is C++20 (spec §4.2); this is the reason D8' confines C++ to `adapters/`.
Forces on later phases:
- Phase 2: Verilog-2005 front end, preprocessor, elaboration (spec §12).
- Phase 5: slang adapter, GHDL path, cross-language identical-netlist test.
- Spec §4.2: the slang shim exports exactly one C function, `odin3_read_slang(...)`, in a
  separate shared library.
