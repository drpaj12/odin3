# Micros the cmos6 gate flow (gatemap-run: abc --gates, dfflibmap, write_blif --gates-as-names
# against the RTL's, equiv-check) cannot decide. Format: `<path> [needle] <reason>`; the needle must
# occur in gatemap-run's output. The micros that proc, opt or lower refuse are in the lists of
# those passes (tests/proc, tests/opt, tests/lower), which the sweep reads too.
# Reasons: multiclock (equiv-check handles one clock domain and one edge per file),
# negedge-inverter (cmos6 has no falling-edge flip-flop, so dfflibmap puts an INV on the clock
# and equiv-check cannot read a flip-flop clocked by an internal net), unproven (no verdict
# within equiv-check's limit or at the 2G child cap), no-latch-cell (cmos6 has no latch cell,
# GM-9). No micro needed init-lost (GM-8).
# Every unproven entry but the 2G-cap ones has the needle `gatemap-run: unproven`: a design near the
# 600 s limit gives either `timed out after 600 seconds` or `UNDECIDED` depending on load. Such an
# entry can XPASS on a faster machine (docs/V1.0.2.md); that is a list update, not a bug.
# Evidence: ctest gatemap_micro_cmos6, release build, 2026-10-22; counts in docs/V1.0.2.md.
